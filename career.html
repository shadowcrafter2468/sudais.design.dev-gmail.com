LIFE AT 10PEARLS

At 10Pearls, we have an inclusive and collaborative work culture that inspires innovation, drives excellence, and unlocks a team member’s true potential through focused development, shared mission, and a sense of community. We value our team as our greatest asset and we ensure everyone is respected, recognized, and celebrated.

So, how is life at 10Pearls? Keep reading to learn more about our work culture and philosophy.

We are lifelong learners

At 10Pearls, learning never stops. Whether it’s a casual conversation, a 10PearlsUniversity bootcamp, or one of our guest speaker sessions conducted by acclaimed local and international speakers, learning is embedded into everything we do. Plus, every learning opportunity is open toall—from trainees to folks who have been with us for 10 years. If you feel a program will benefit you, you are more than welcome to be a part of it.

I have drastically improved my soft skills by attending various hands-on trainings. I am more confident and have stronger relationships with clients and my colleagues.

We thrive on challenges

As a company, we have experienced exponential growth—and this is largely due to our enthusiasm to take on challenging and complex projects. We’re constantly pushing the envelope, exploring innovative solutions, and disrupting the status quo. From large, globally distributed solutions to small-scale startups, retail to health sector, modernization to emerging tech, our scope of work is vast, diverse, and constantly evolving!

The exposure I have received here is unmatched. Each project is unique, complex, and gives me an opportunity to do something new.

We embrace an open work culture

Our open-door policy encourages dialogue and communication between all our employees, across departments and locations. Every team member can easily access any of the senior management to discuss an idea, raise a concern, or seek guidance. We foster this feeling of openness not only through regular all-staff meetings and team sessions with our co-founders, but also by simply hanging out and eating together! With zero tolerance for counterproductive work politics, we take pride in providing a truly employee-centric culture.

It’s inspiring to know that I am trusted and given ownership of my work. I know that I am contributing to our company’s growth, and that really motivates me.

We bring the power of diversity to life

We believe there is transformative power in engaging different perspectives. We foster a culture where people of diverse backgrounds and experiences feel recognized and comfortable to be who they are, and feel inspired to do their very best. We are committed to having a diverse, equity-focused, and inclusive culture and provide a conducive environment for our team to feel empowered to grow.

It’s heartwarming to be celebrated for who I am and to absorb diverse viewpoints. All this has given me invaluable exposure, empathy, and deeper perspective to create better and inclusive solutions.
